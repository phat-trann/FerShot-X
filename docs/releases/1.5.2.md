# FerShot X 1.5.2

- Library reopens centered at 75% of the usable area on the display under the pointer, including after manual resize or consecutive opens.
- Record Area starts without the dimming overlay that could prevent ScreenCaptureKit from starting. Region selection, Stop controls and playback in Edit remain available.
- Live Text wheel and pinch zoom anchor at the pointer and preserve native text selection.
- Library refreshes only the source that changed, keeps previous pixels out of a new selection and retains unrelated FTP previews in cache. Recording settings errors stay within their section.

Native window placement and capture remain installed acceptance checks. [Recording checklist](video-manual-acceptance.md) · [Live Text checklist](live-text-manual-acceptance.md)
